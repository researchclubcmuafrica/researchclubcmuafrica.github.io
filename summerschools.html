All times are Kigali time (CAT, UTC+2). What about AoE and OpenReview?

CAT is Central Africa Time, which is UTC+2 (UTC means Coordinated Universal Time). Many conferences give deadlines in AoE, Anywhere on Earth (UTC−12). This means the deadline has passed only when it is over everywhere in the world. AoE is 14 hours behind Kigali, so 23:59 AoE on 7 December is 13:59 on 8 December in Kigali. Some cards also show when the OpenReview submission form closes (OpenReview is the website many conferences use to collect papers). This is often the abstract or registration deadline, a few days before the full paper deadline. Always check the official page before you submit.

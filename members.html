Faculty Advisor

Distinguished faculty member who provide guidance and mentorship to our research community.

Prof. Timothy X. Brown

Professor, Electrical and Computer Engineering, and Engineering and Public Policy;
Director, Kigali Collaborative Research Centre

Electrical and Computer Engineering / Engineering and Public Policy

Prof. Timothy X. Brown, Ph.D. (Caltech), leads CMU-Africa’s research initiatives and fosters collaborations with industry and academia. His research focuses on adaptive network control, machine learning, and wireless communication systems. As Director of the Kigali Collaborative Research Centre, he supports research partnerships, mentorship, and innovation across the CMU-Africa community.
